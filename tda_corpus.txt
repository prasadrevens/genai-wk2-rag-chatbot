PART 2 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Find answers to common questions about our classes and policies.


1. What should I wear to class?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wear comfortable, breathable clothing that allows you to move freely. Athletic
wear like leggings, t-shirts, or tank tops work great. Avoid jeans or
restrictive clothing. We dance barefoot or in dance socks, so no special shoes
are required.


2. Do I need any prior dance experience?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not at all! Our classes are designed for all experience levels. Our instructors
break down moves step by step, so even complete beginners can follow along and
have fun. We focus on enjoyment and fitness, not perfection.


3. What is BollyX?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BollyX is a Bollywood-inspired dance fitness program that combines dynamic
choreography with the latest music from around the world. It's a high-energy
cardio workout disguised as a dance party! No dance experience necessary.


4. How do I enroll in a class?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply browse our class schedule, select the class you're interested in, and
click 'Enroll Now'. You'll fill out a short form with your information and
complete the payment through our secure checkout. You'll receive a confirmation
email with all the details.


5. What is your cancellation/refund policy?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our 4-class session enrollments are non-refundable. If a class is canceled by
the academy, it is not counted and an automatic class credit is applied, with
your enrollment end date extended accordingly. In some cases a missed group
class may be made up in another batch while your enrollment is still active.
For any special circumstances, please reach out and we'll connect you with the
team to review your situation.


6. Can I try a class before committing?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Absolutely! We occasionally offer trial classes or drop-in sessions. Contact us
to learn about current opportunities to try out a class before enrolling in a
full session.


7. What age groups do you serve?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We offer classes for all ages! Our programs include Kids Dance (ages 4-7 and
7-12), Teen Dance (ages 12-18), Adult Ladies Dance, and BollyX Fitness
(ages 16+). There's something for everyone in the family!


8. Where are classes held?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our classes are held at the Melissa Community Center in Melissa, Texas. The
address and room details will be provided in your enrollment confirmation email.
