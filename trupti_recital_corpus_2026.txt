=== WHO CAN PERFORM ===

Two groups can perform in the Annual Recital:

1. Internal students — anyone currently enrolled in a Trupti Dance
   Academy class for the 2025–2026 session. Internal students are
   eligible to register for the recital but are not automatically
   entered; a separate recital registration is required.

2. External / community performers — dancers who are NOT enrolled in
   regular classes at the academy, including students from other
   studios, family groups, and independent performers. External
   performers are welcome and register through the same form, at a
   different fee tier (see Registration & Fees).

All performers, internal and external, must complete recital
registration and attend the mandatory dress rehearsal to perform.


=== PERFORMER CATEGORIES (2026 RECITAL) ===

The first recital's routines were organized into age- and group-based
categories. Performers were grouped as follows:

- Kids (4–6 years) — youngest solo/group dancers.
- Kids (7–8 years).
- Girls group (9–15 years).
- Ladies group (15+ years) — teen and adult women.
- Parent and child (2–3 years) — a parent dancing together with a
  toddler aged 2–3.

Routines included both group numbers and solo performances across these
categories. A performer's category was based on the dancer's age (and,
for the parent-and-child category, on the parent-plus-toddler pairing).

=== RECITAL REFUND & WITHDRAWAL POLICY ===

This refund policy applies ONLY to the Annual Recital. It is separate
from, and different from, the refund rules for regular class
enrollments. Class enrollment refunds are governed by the academy's
Terms & Conditions, not by this section.

For the recital specifically:

1. Participation fee — refundable, minus a $15 administrative fee, if
   the performer withdraws in writing at least 30 days before the
   recital date. Withdrawals fewer than 30 days before the recital are
   not eligible for a participation-fee refund, because venue and
   staffing costs are already committed. [VERIFY amounts/window]

2. Costume fee — non-refundable once costumes have been ordered.
   Costumes are ordered immediately after the registration deadline.
   A performer who withdraws after costumes are ordered still receives
   their costume but does not receive a costume-fee refund.

3. Audience tickets — non-refundable, but transferable to another
   guest.

4. Removal for missed dress rehearsal — treated as a withdrawal as of
   the recital date, so the participation fee is non-refundable at that
   point and the costume fee follows rule 2.

Special situations — medical emergencies, injury, or family
emergencies — are reviewed individually. Performers in these situations
should contact the academy directly so the team can help.


=== RECITAL FAQ ===

Q: When is the recital? / Is there a recital coming up?
A: Trupti Dance Academy held its first Annual Recital on May 30, 2026,
from 6:00 to 8:00 PM, and that event is now complete. There isn't a
new recital date announced yet. The academy plans to make the recital
an annual tradition, so watch for an announcement about the next one,
or contact the team to be notified.

Q: How did the first recital go?
A: Wonderfully — it was the academy's first-ever recital, with 60+
performers in both group and solo routines, and a strong, enthusiastic
turnout of parents and community members.

Q: Can I still register for the recital?
A: Registration for the 2026 recital is closed and the event has
already happened. There is no open recital registration right now. When
the next recital is announced, registration details will be shared.

Q: What age groups perform in the recital? / Is there something for
very young children?
A: The first recital had categories spanning all ages: Kids (4–6),
Kids (7–8), a Girls group (9–15), a Ladies group (15+), and a Parent
and child category for toddlers aged 2–3 dancing with a parent. So
even very young children can take part — the youngest dance alongside a
parent.

Q: Is the recital included in my class fees?
A: No. The recital is a separate optional event with its own
registration and fees. Being enrolled in a class makes you eligible to
register, but the recital participation fee and costume fee are charged
separately.

Q: My child takes classes here — are they automatically in the recital?
A: No. Enrolled students are eligible but must complete a separate
recital registration by the deadline to perform.

Q: Can my niece, who dances at another studio, perform in the recital?
A: Yes. External and community performers are welcome and register
through the same form at the external fee tier.

Q: Can I get a refund if my child can't do the recital after all?
A: It depends on when and on which fee. The recital has its own refund
rules: the participation fee is refundable minus an admin fee if you
withdraw at least 30 days before the recital, and the costume fee is
non-refundable once costumes are ordered. These recital rules are
different from the refund rules for regular classes. For your specific
situation, the team can walk you through what applies.

Q: How many tickets do performers get?
A: Each registered performer receives complimentary guest tickets;
additional guests need audience tickets.

Q: What if we miss the dress rehearsal?
A: The dress rehearsal is mandatory to perform. Missing it without
prior arrangement may mean being removed from the show, and recital
fees then follow the recital refund policy. If something comes up,
contact the team as early as possible.
